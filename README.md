### 🐾 Belemonai

A multiplayer virtual-pet game built around **long-term progression, customization, minigames, collection systems and a player-driven economy**.

Each player has their own **Belemonas** — a personal pet that can be developed and customized over time rather than being a fixed character.

The project includes systems around:

* pet care and progression
* evolutions
* cosmetic customization
* wearable and collectible items
* player-specific visual combinations
* in-game marketplace and shop systems
* currencies and progression loops
* minigames
* multiplayer-oriented activities
* collection mechanics
* persistent player state

A major part of the project is making every player's Belemonas feel individual, with a large modular customization system rather than a small set of predefined skins.

The game world is also designed around shared locations and activities. One example is **Centrinė Aikštė**, which acts as a hub for larger gameplay systems such as:

* **Šulinio Žvejyba** — a fishing, grinding, collection and economy system
* **Kiemo Bylos** — a detective and treasure-hunt system where players gather clues, explore locations, solve cases and progress through detective ranks

Belemonai is one of my larger experiments in combining **game systems, persistent progression, customization, social mechanics and economy design** into one connected product.
